# DangerMod
Danger Mod

Danger Mod is an ambitious weather and hazard expansion for Kerbal Space Program. Designed to make atmospheric flight more challenging, immersive, and unpredictable, Danger Mod introduces dynamic weather threats that can turn an ordinary mission into a fight for survival.

Unlike traditional KSP gameplay, where Kerbin's atmosphere is mostly calm and predictable, Danger Mod aims to create a world where pilots must constantly monitor conditions, react to unexpected dangers, and make critical decisions during flight.

Vision

The long-term goal of Danger Mod is to transform Kerbin into a more realistic and dangerous environment. Pilots should feel the need to monitor conditions, prepare for unexpected events, and adapt their missions to changing weather patterns.

Whether launching a small aircraft, flying a massive SSTO, or sending a rocket into orbit, every mission should carry a sense of uncertainty.
